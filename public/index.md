# CollegeCart

CollegeCart is a campus convenience delivery platform in India. It delivers
groceries, dairy, snacks and daily essentials to college hostel rooms in about
**10 minutes**. Founded by **Alok Mishra** in 2025. Live at **Shivalik College**
and **Quantum University**.

## What you can order

Fruits and vegetables, atta, rice and dal, oil and ghee, masala, dairy and eggs,
bread and bakery, chips and namkeen, sweets and chocolates, cold drinks and
juices, tea and coffee, instant food, home and kitchen essentials and personal
care.

## How delivery works

1. Pick your hostel and room once — it is remembered for every later order.
2. Fill your cart. There is no minimum order value.
3. A rider brings it to your hostel block in about 10 minutes.

Orders are packed from our own dark store, so the price and stock shown on the
product page is what actually ships. Stock is refreshed on the shop page every
minute.

## Coverage

- Shivalik College — Dehradun, Uttarakhand
- Quantum University — Roorkee, Uttarakhand

## Payments

UPI, credit card, debit card, netbanking and wallets via Razorpay, plus cash on
delivery where it is available for your hostel.

## Missing or damaged items

Raise a request from the order screen. Missing, damaged or wrong items are
refunded to the original payment method.
